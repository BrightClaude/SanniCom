How I work

A clear process,
every step of the way.

Six steps from the first audit to a store that sells more. You always know what we’re fixing, why it matters and how it’s performing.

  1. STEP 01

    Audit

    01

    Audit your store and data

    I dig into your store analytics, email and SMS performance and customer journey to find where visitors drop off and where revenue is leaking.

  2. STEP 02

    Strategy

    02

    Build your revenue roadmap

    Prioritised flows, campaigns, offers and segments, tied to clear goals like conversion rate, repeat purchase rate and customer lifetime value.

  3. STEP 03

    Creative

    03

    Craft on-brand campaigns

    Email and SMS creative, offers and product-focused layouts that fit your brand and make buying effortless on mobile.

  4. STEP 04

    Build

    04

    Set up flows and integrations

    Welcome, abandoned cart, post-purchase and win-back flows built in Klaviyo and connected to your Shopify store, with segments, forms and tracking.

  5. STEP 05

    Launch

    05

    Test and go live

    Every flow is QA’d across devices and inboxes, with deliverability checks and A/B tests, before it goes live, ideally ahead of peak season.

  6. STEP 06

    Scale

    06

    Measure, optimise, repeat

    Monthly reporting on revenue per recipient, repeat purchase rate and flow performance, plus a plan for BFCM and your next growth push.
